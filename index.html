you found the light

I've left social media, but I left a beacon behind. The accounts you followed still send out a signal, and every one of them points here, so you can find me now that I'm gone.

↓  read on

Deleting everything felt like slipping out the back door. A lot of people I care about still live in those feeds, and I didn't want to just vanish on them. So I left a light on. Follow it and you'll find me somewhere quieter: in my letters, around a table, and in the things I'm still making. — BC

A note on the signal

My accounts and Runaway Anchor's keep posting on their own: new letters, new drinks and the occasional classic from the archive. Each post is a small flash of the beacon, pointing back here. Nobody reads the messages or comments there. If you want to reach me, reply to any letter or email bc@runawayanchor.com. I do write back.
